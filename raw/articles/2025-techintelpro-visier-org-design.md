# Visier Org Design: AI-Powered Workforce Planning at HR Tech 2025

- by:
- |
- September 19, 2025
Visier, a leader in Workforce Intelligence, has introduced an innovative approach to organizational design, leveraging AI and rich analytics to transform workforce planning. Announced at HR Tech 2025, this solution empowers transformation leaders to make data-driven decisions, enhancing agility and performance across organizations.
- 
Visier Org Design, launched at HR Tech 2025, streamlines workforce planning with AI-powered insights.
- 
Integrates analytics, planning, and organizational design for informed decision-making.
- 
Enables modeling of future-state organizational structures with ease and precision.
- 
Evaluates impacts of changes on costs, skills, engagement, and performance metrics.
- 
Tracks real-time progress of restructuring plans within Visier’s ecosystem.
- 
Designed for scalability, from team realignments to enterprise-wide transformations.
Visier Org Design, unveiled at HR Tech 2025 in Las Vegas, redefines workforce planning by integrating AI-driven analytics into organizational design. Unlike traditional tools that focus solely on org charts, this solution combines people data with analytics to provide insights into costs, skills, engagement, and performance. With GenAI narrating the impact of changes, leaders can make strategic decisions to optimize talent density and drive business success.
In today’s fast-evolving business environment, workforce planning requires continuous adaptability. Visier Org Design supports dynamic, data-backed restructuring at any scale, from local team adjustments to enterprise-wide reorganizations. It leverages validated and harmonized people data within Visier’s secure ecosystem, enabling leaders to model scenarios and monitor outcomes effectively. The solution’s collaborative canvas simplifies planning with built-in logic for roles, layers, and spans of control.
"Every reorg is a business-critical decision," said Keith Bigelow, Senior VP & Chief Product Officer of Visier. "Until now, leaders have had to rely on spreadsheets, siloed tools, or months-long projects to explore organizational changes, and even then future structures were often a guessing game. Visier Org Design brings this capability in-house, and with Vee Boards leaders receive a narrated analysis of their changes to catch high risk and non-compliant actions before they happen. With the proactive foresight Visier Org Design enables, we're helping companies make dramatically better, faster decisions about how to achieve talent density."
Visier Org Design allows leaders to evaluate the downstream effects of proposed changes instantly. By comparing current and future-state scenarios, users can assess metrics like compensation costs, attrition risks, and employee performance. This ensures organizational changes align with strategic goals, minimizing risks and maximizing outcomes.
The solution enables real-time tracking of restructuring plans within Visier’s ecosystem. Leaders can monitor execution, compare actuals against plans, and ensure changes deliver intended business value. This continuous feedback loop supports agile decision-making and long-term success.
"The future of work demands that businesses treat organizational transformation not as a periodic, high-risk event, but as a continuous, strategic capability," said Marc Solow, Managing Director and Digital Workplace Practice Leader, Deloitte Consulting LLP. "This requires democratizing access to data and insights beyond the executive suite. We see tools like Visier Org Design as essential enablers of this shift, empowering leaders at every level to model scenarios, understand downstream impacts, and execute change with a clarity and speed that was previously unattainable."
Visier Org Design empowers organizations to build agile, data-driven workforce strategies, transforming organizational design into a measurable, strategic capability. For more details, visit Visier at HR Tech 2025, Booth #5209.
