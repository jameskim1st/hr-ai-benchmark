# HR Acuity Reviews & Ratings 2026 | Gartner Peer Insights

HR Acuity is a software that provides organizations with tools to manage employee relations and workplace investigations. The software enables documentation of employee issues, tracks case progress, and maintains a centralized record of all related information. It supports consistent application of workplace policies by offering workflows for managing incidents, investigations, and follow-up actions. HR Acuity also offers analytics and reporting features to help organizations identify trends and risks related to employee relations. The software is designed to assist HR teams in ensuring compliance with internal procedures and external regulations, improving transparency, and reducing organizational risk associated with employee incidents.
