# PwC's HR, tech leaders prepare to train U.S. workforce on ChatGPT technology

## Summary

HR Executive 기사(Dawn Kawamoto, 2023-05-16). PwC가 미국 직원 65,000명 이상을 대상으로 3년에 걸쳐 ChatGPT·생성형 AI 기초~심화 교육을 계획, $1 billion AI 투자의 일부로 2023년 여름 시작 예정. CPO Yolanda Seals-Coffield와 부회장 겸 CPTO Joe Atkinson 공동 주도. 직무·책임에 따라 교육 수준을 차등화하고 live training·gamification 등으로 전달 계획. 책임 있는 사용·프롬프트 작성 교육 포함. Microsoft 파트너십(ChatGPT·Azure OpenAI Service)은 클라이언트 대상 제공물. 2026-10-19 Jina로 원문 확보

## Key Quotes

> Professional services and accounting powerhouse PwC is looking to power up its U.S. workforce of over 65,000 employees with basic to advanced knowledge of [ChatGPT technology](https://www.hrexecutive.com/chatgpt-bigger-than-anything-hr-has-ever-seen/) over the next three years, and it's putting big bucks behind that effort.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 미국 직원 65,000명 이상, 3년 계획 (2023-05 계획 시점)

> PwC [recently announced](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/pwc-us-makes-billion-investment-in-ai-capabilities.html) that it's making a $1 billion investment to super-size and scale its AI capabilities. Part of that initiative is upskilling its entire U.S. workforce on generative AI, of which ChatGPT is just [one form](https://www.cio.com/article/474809/chatgpt-the-rise-of-generative-ai.html), beginning this summer. It's a mammoth training effort led by both PwC's Chief People Officer Yolanda Seals-Coffield, who calls it a "fundamental" need for all employees, and also the company's vice chair and chief products and technology officer Joe Atkinson.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: $1 billion AI 투자의 일부로 전 미국 직원 업스킬링, CPO·CPTO 공동 오너십

> The investment also includes a partnership with Microsoft to create scalable offerings using both OpenAI's [ChatGPT](https://www.hrexecutive.com/workers-are-already-using-chatgpt-how-can-hr-catch-up/) and Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service to help PwC's clients leverage generative AI technology.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 기술 스택(ChatGPT·Azure OpenAI Service)은 클라이언트 제공물 맥락

> PwC plans to assess which is the most relevant generative AI information to provide to employees, based on their responsibilities and job description. The company also plans to deliver the materials and information through live training sessions, gamification and other means.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 직무별 차등 교육과 전달 방식 (계획)

> One particular area of importance in upskilling PwC employees will be showing them how to use generative AI responsibly and transparently so everyone is aware of exactly what is being done, Atkinson says.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 책임 있는 AI 사용 교육 포함
