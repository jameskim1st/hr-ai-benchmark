# Top Indian IT Companies Retain Their Workforce and Promote AI Skill Retraining

## Summary

AIbase 뉴스(2025-10-20). FY2026 2분기 기준 TCS·Infosys·HCLTech·Wipro의 채용 둔화와 이직률 안정(Wipro 14.9%, HCL 12.6%, Infosys 14.3%, TCS 13.3% — 5년 최저)을 전하며 AI가 HR 전략의 중심이 됐다고 보도. TCS 사내 이니셔티브 'Ideate and Build with AI'에 275,000명 참여, TCS 직원 수는 613,069명→593,314명으로 감소.

## Key Quotes

> TCS's internal innovation initiative "Ideate and Build with AI" has achieved significant success, with 275,000 employees participating in this unprecedented upskilling program aimed at integrating AI literacy into all functions of the company.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: TCS 275,000명 'Ideate and Build with AI' 참여

> In the latest data, Wipro's employee turnover rate was 14.9%, HCL was 12.6%, Infosys was 14.3%, and TCS was 13.3%.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 4사 이직률(FY26 Q2)

> Despite this, TCS's total number of employees decreased from 613,069 in the previous quarter to 593,314
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: TCS 인력 감소
