# How AtkinsRéalis Uses A Skills-based Approach To Secure Talent That Drives World-Changing Innovations

## Summary
Beamery의 AtkinsRéalis($8.6B 엔지니어링 기업) 고객 사례. Workday와 연동해 skills-based 채용으로 전환한 결과, Beamery 경유 채용자가 성과 마일스톤을 25% 빨리 달성, best-fit 후보 식별 시간 30% 단축, 후보 engagement 점수 40% 상승을 주장. AtkinsRéalis의 Shijo Thomas 인용 포함.

## Key Quotes
> AtkinsRéalis is an $8.6B engineering organization, connecting people, data and technology to transform the world’s infrastructure and energy systems.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AtkinsRéalis 규모($8.6B)

> The skills-based approach Beamery facilitates has also improved the quality of hires, with new hires sourced through Beamery achieving their performance milestones 25% faster than non-Beamery sourced hires.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 신규 채용자 성과 마일스톤 25% 빠른 도달

> - 30% reduction in time to identify best-fit candidates

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: best-fit 후보 식별 시간 30% 단축

> - 40% increase in candidate engagement scores, driven by more personalized communications

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 후보 engagement 40% 상승
