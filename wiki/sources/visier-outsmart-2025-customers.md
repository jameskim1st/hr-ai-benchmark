## Summary

Visier's Outsmart 2025 annual customer conference (400+ attendees) showcased three named Vizzie Award winners with disclosed metrics. Visier also launched MCP for AI agents (2026-04) enabling governed people data across enterprise AI stacks. Corroborated by Visier press release (Tier 4) + analyst coverage (Brandon Hall Group, Tier 2).

## Named Customer Case Studies

### 1. Tampa General Hospital — Business Performance Impact Award
- **12,000 full-time employees**, large US health system
- **Challenge**: Excessive agency labor / travel nurse spend
- **Solution**: Visier people analytics to identify vacancy root causes and investment redirects
- **Results**:
  - Agency labor spend reduced by **70%** (eliminated inpatient travel nurses)
  - Vacancy rate dropped below **10%**
  - **$50M+** reinvested into team member compensation (retention/rehires)
- **Related**: Tampa General HIMSS Stage 7 Analytics certification (June 2025, first in Florida); 61 AI applications deployed enterprise-wide

### 2. Docusign — Adoption & Analytics Excellence Award
- **Challenge**: People analytics not embedded in business units
- **Solution**: Innovative dashboards, tailored training, automated reporting via Visier
- **Results**:
  - Visier active users: **+75%**
  - Total sessions: **+132%**
  - Manual reporting eliminated across 9 business units

### 3. Johnson & Johnson — Cross-functional Data Alignment
- **Challenge**: Gap between HR and Finance headcount data
- **Solution**: Unified headcount reconciliation via Visier
- **Results**: Created single source of truth for headcount; specific financial metrics not disclosed
