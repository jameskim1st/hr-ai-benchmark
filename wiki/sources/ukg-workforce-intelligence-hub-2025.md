## Workforce Intelligence Hub (Launched November 2025)

- **Function**: Real-time unified view of all workforce data for executives and managers
- **AI capabilities**:
  - Demand forecasting (staffing need prediction)
  - Unexpected overtime spike detection
  - Real-time shift coverage and labor gap visibility
  - Dynamic Labor Management (predictive staffing)
- **Scale**: 80,000+ organizations, 150 countries; 12+ billion schedules created; 10 billion punches recorded
