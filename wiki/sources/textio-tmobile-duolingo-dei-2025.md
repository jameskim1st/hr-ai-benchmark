## Summary

Textio published two named customer case studies with specific metrics: T-Mobile and Duolingo. Coverage also noted in HR Brew (March 2025) for new skills-based interview tools. Both case studies are self-reported via Textio's website (Tier 3 — vendor primary). T-Mobile case also referenced in ai.business coverage (Tier 4). No Tier 1/2 independent audit of the DEI outcome metrics found.

## T-Mobile Case Study

- **Scale**: ~125 recruiters + Employer Brand Marketing + DEI employees + 9,000+ hiring managers
- **Integration**: Textio embedded directly into Workday ATS
- **Context**: Deployed during company's major merger integration

### Results (⚠️ 자사 보고 / Textio vendor claim)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Women applicants (with gender-neutral tone) | +17% |
| Time-to-fill (posts at Textio Score ≥90) | 5 days faster |
| Scope | Job posts, recruiting emails, employer brand content |

## Duolingo Case Study

- **Starting state**: Textio Score of 54 on job descriptions
- **Policy**: Require all job posts to score ≥85 before posting; currently averaging ~90

### Results (⚠️ 자사 보고 / Textio vendor claim)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Textio Score improvement | 54 → ~90 |
| Posting policy | All posts must score ≥85 |
| Standardization | Boilerplate/intro language standardized company-wide |
| Candidate feedback | Described as "less jargony," "more Duolingo brand" |
| Representation tracking | Track at every hiring funnel stage |

## Textio Platform Aggregate Metrics (⚠️ 벤더 주장)

- Organizations using Textio: 16% more email responses, 1.5x more qualified candidates, 12% more women applicants
- Textio-optimized posts: +17% average applications, +23% from underrepresented groups
