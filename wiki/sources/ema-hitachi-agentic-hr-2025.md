## Summary

Hitachi built an agentic AI HR companion called **Skye** on Ema's platform, covering 20+ use cases across the employee lifecycle (hire-to-retire). The initial deployment targeted a shared HR services arm supporting 40,000+ employees across five business units in the US, Japan, and Europe. The project started in early 2025. Primary coverage by HR Executive (Tier 2) corroborates Ema's own vendor case study (Tier 3).
