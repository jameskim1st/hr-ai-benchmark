# Accenture and Walmart are developing workforce strategies with AI in mind

## Summary
HR Brew가 2025-10-02 게재한 기사. Accenture CEO Julie Sweet의 "reskilling이 불가능한 인력은 compressed timeline으로 exit"라는 발언과, AI·데이터 인력(약 77,000명) 확대 및 2025년 약 22,000명 감원을 다루며, Walmart도 유사한 AI 중심 인력 전략을 취하고 있다고 보도(WSJ 인용). 본 요약은 WebSearch 결과 스니펫에 기반하며 원문은 직접 확보하지 못했다.

## Key Quotes

> “Our number one strategy is upskilling,” Sweet said on the call. “We are exiting on a compressed timeline, people, where reskilling, based on our experience, is not a viable path for the skills we need.”
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Accenture의 upskilling 우선 전략과 reskilling 불가 인력의 compressed timeline exit (CEO Julie Sweet, 실적 콜)

> Sweet went on to say that the company, which has 779,000 employees globally, is restructuring to be an AI-driven business. While Accenture has cut roughly 22,000 employees so far in 2025, it isn’t looking to reduce its overall headcount. Instead, the company is focused on adding to its AI and data workforce of about 77,000 in the hopes of saving at least $1 billion that it intends to reinvest in workers, CFO Angie Park said on the call.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 직원 779,000명, 2025년 약 22,000명 감원, AI·데이터 인력 약 77,000명, $1B 절감 후 인력 재투자

> Accenture has also been hard at work training current employees for a future with GenAI and has trained more than half a million employees.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: GenAI 교육 이수 직원 50만 명 이상

> Walmart is taking a similar strategy with its workforce, according to the Wall Street Journal. “It’s very clear that AI is going to change literally every job,” CEO Doug McMillon recently told business leaders during a workforce conference at the retailer’s Bentonville headquarters. Walmart expects to cut some roles and tasks and create new ones, without changing its overall headcount.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Walmart의 AI 중심 인력 전략, 총 headcount 유지 방침 (WSJ 재인용)

> McMillon told the Associated Press that he expects Walmart’s corporate roles to be impacted by AI faster than its store and fulfillment jobs, though he’s unsure exactly what that will look like and how the changes will take place.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 사무직이 매장·물류직보다 먼저 영향받을 것이라는 McMillon 전망 (AP 재인용)
