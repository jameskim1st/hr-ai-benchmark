## Summary

Perceptyx published three named customer case studies as 2025 EX Impact Award winners. Perceptyx serves 600+ global enterprises including one-third of Fortune 100. Award for "Talent Management — Best Advance in Practical AI" won by Perceptyx Activate (2025 HR Tech Awards). Case studies are self-reported by customer companies but published via Perceptyx (Tier 2 HR tech media-adjacent). Independent Forrester recognition noted.

## Customer Case Studies

### 1. Arca Continental Coca-Cola Southwest Beverages (AC-CCSWB)
- 9,000+ associates, Dallas TX, largest US Coca-Cola bottler
- **Deployed**: Perceptyx Activate with Intelligent Nudges (AI-generated coaching prompts based on engagement results)
- **Results (2020–2025 longitudinal)**:
  - Leadership Index favorability: 65% → 89.3% (+24.3 pp over 5 years)
  - 1,191 action plans created / 1,871 activities (2024 annual survey cycle)
  - 93% of leaders created action plans
  - Won 2025 Coca-Cola Candler Cup (global bottler excellence award)

### 2. Emerson
- Global manufacturing/automation company
- **Deployed**: Well-being Champions Network + Perceptyx listening
- **Results (1 year)**:
  - Well-being index score: 71% → 83% (+12 pp)
  - Champion-led teams: 90.5% engagement favorability
  - Leader trust: 13.2% higher than rest of org
  - Intent to leave: 22% lower in Champion-led teams
  - Network: ~200 Well-being Champions across geographies

### 3. Advocate Health
- Large US health system
- **Deployed**: Unified feedback system (consolidation of multiple surveys)
- **Results**:
  - Survey questions reduced from 250 → 49
  - 6,000 leaders trained in action planning
  - Additional details not disclosed

## Entities
