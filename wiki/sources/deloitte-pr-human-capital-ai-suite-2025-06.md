# Deloitte Launches AI Solution Suite for Human and Machine Workforce

## Summary
Deloitte 2025-06-24 보도자료. Human Capital AI 솔루션 suite 출시 — Workforce Analyzer(GenAI로 직무별 AI 영향·disruption 평가), Workforce Planner+(AI 기반 인력 수급 분석·시나리오), HR AI Maturity Model·Diagnostic·Agentic AI Workflow Library(전략·데이터·기술·거버넌스·솔루션 딜리버리·인력 enablement 6개 차원). Salesforce의 Ruth Hickin(VP Workforce Innovation and Transformation)이 고객으로 인용됨.

> NEW YORK, June 24, 2025 — Deloitte announced the launch of its Human Capital AI solution suite, featuring Workforce Analyzer and Workforce Planner+.

> Workforce Analyzer enables leaders to assess work functions, estimate AI disruption potential in roles, and develop scenarios that enhance efficiency and support growth.
