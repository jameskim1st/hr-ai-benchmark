# How HSBC powered a future-fit skills agenda | Gloat

## Summary
Gloat의 HSBC 고객 성공 사례. 22만 명 규모 조직에서 수작업 재배치의 한계를 계기로 Gloat 탤런트 마켓플레이스를 도입, 약 60,000 unlocked hours, 프로젝트의 45%가 cross-functional, 구조조정 대상 직원의 25%가 새 기회를 찾았다는 성과를 제시. Group Head of Resourcing Hamish Nisbet 인터뷰 기반.

## Key Quotes
> ~60,000 unlocked hours 45% of projects are cross-functional 25% of restructured employees found new opportunities
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: ⚠️ 벤더 보고 핵심 성과 3종

> “We looked at redeployments, but they were really manual. It required too much human intervention. It wasn’t systemized. And with an organization like ours, with over 220,000 employees, it’s really difficult to get that to any sort of scale,” Nisbet notes.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Before: 수작업 재배치 한계, 220,000명

> As their Group Head of Resourcing Hamish Nisbet explains, “We were wrestling with how, as an organization, we ensure we have the right people with the right skills at the right time.”
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 도입 동기: 스킬 기반 인력 배치
