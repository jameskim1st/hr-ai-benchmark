# Future workforce: CBA launches new skills and careers program

## Summary
CBA 2026-02 보도자료. 3년간 약 A$9,000만 투자의 Future Workforce Program 발표. 지난 1년간 약 5,000명이 사내 새 역할로 이동했고 30,000명 이상에게 AI 교육 제공. 신규 'Grow Your Career' 플랫폼으로 역할별 전환 가능 스킬·수요 스킬을 시각화하고, 인력 변화 영향 직원에게 강화된 커리어 전환 지원 제공.

## Key Quotes
> Commonwealth Bank today announced a new skills program to prepare its workforce for the changing nature of work, supported by approximately $90 million investment over three years.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: $90M·3년 프로그램

> Over the past year, approximately 5,000 of our existing employees started in a new role inside the bank, and we rolled out a comprehensive suite of AI tools and have provided AI-focused training to more than 30,000 of our people.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 내부 이동 5,000명·AI 교육 30,000명+

> Through the new Grow Your Career platform employees will be able to see how technology can support aspects of their work, the transferable skills they are developing, and how to build new in-demand skills. The Program will also integrate enhanced career transition support for people impacted by workforce change.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Grow Your Career 플랫폼·전환 지원
