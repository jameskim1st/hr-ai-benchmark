# Hirevue leads industry in fair and ethical hiring practice, engaging external auditor DCI Consulting Group for external bias audit of algorithms

## Summary
HireVue가 2023년 1월 워싱턴 DC 소재 컴플라이언스 컨설팅사 DCI Consulting Group에 자사 알고리즘의 외부 편향 감사를 의뢰했다는 보도자료. NYC Local Law 144 제안 규칙에 따라 역량 기반·게임 기반 알고리즘을 인종·성별·교차 조합 기준으로 다수 직급·use case에 걸쳐 감사하며, 결과는 HireVue 웹사이트에 공개 예정. HireVue는 이전에도 다수의 독립 감사와 AI 설명가능성 성명서를 발간했다고 밝힌다.

## Key Quotes
> January 28, 2023, Salt Lake City, UTAH: HireVue, the global leader in video interviewing, AI-based assessments, and text-enabled recruiting tools, has engaged DCI Consulting Group (DCI), a leading Washington DC based human resources compliance and data analytics consulting firm, to audit its algorithms in accordance with proposed rules specified by New York City Local Law 144.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: DCI Consulting 외부 bias audit 의뢰

> Competency-based and game-based algorithms will be audited for bias with respect to race, gender and the intersectional combination of race and gender across multiple job levels and use cases. The audits began in January 2023 and results will be available on the HireVue website once the audit is completed.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 감사 범위(인종·성별·교차)

> HireVue has previously commissioned a number of independent audits of its technology, along with an AI explainability statement – all of which provide customers, candidates and regulators with transparent and meaningful, easy-to-understand information regarding HireVue technology and the processing of personal data.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 이전 독립 감사·설명가능성 성명서 이력
