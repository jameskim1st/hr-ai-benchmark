# Workday Rising 2024: AI Illuminate, AI agents, Evisort acquisition

## Summary

Constellation Research 뉴스 분석(2024-09-17). Workday Rising 2024에서 발표된 차세대 AI 플랫폼 Workday Illuminate(Workday 플랫폼의 8,000억 건 트랜잭션 기반 모델), HR·재무 AI 에이전트 4종(Recruiter·Expenses·Succession·Optimize), 새 인터페이스, Evisort 인수를 정리. GenAI·에이전트가 애플리케이션의 새 UI가 될 수 있다는 관점 제시.

## Key Quotes

> Workday outlined Workday Illuminate, its next-generation AI systems, AI agents across HR and financial processes and new interfaces. In addition, Workday acquired Evisort.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Workday Rising 2024 발표 요약

> Workday announced its next-gen AI platform, Workday Illuminate. Illuminate models are built on 800 billion transactions across Workday's platform.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Illuminate 학습 데이터 규모(8,000억 트랜잭션)

> Workday launched four new AI agents across HR and finance functions. The agents-- Recruiter, Expenses, Succession, and Workday Optimize Agents--are geared to automate workflows.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 초기 AI 에이전트 4종
