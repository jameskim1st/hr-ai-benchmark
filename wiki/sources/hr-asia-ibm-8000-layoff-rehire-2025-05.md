# IBM lays off 8,000 to embrace AI, only to rehire just as many

## Summary
HR Asia가 WSJ의 IBM CEO Arvind Krishna 인터뷰를 인용해 IBM이 AI(AskHR 등) 도입으로 약 8,000명을 감원했으나 절감분으로 프로그래머·영업 인력을 비슷한 규모로 재채용했다고 보도한 기사(제목·검색 요약 기준). 2026-09-27 기준 Cloudflare 차단으로 본문을 확보하지 못했다.

## Key Quotes

> In 2023, IBM made headlines by letting go of nearly 8,000 employees, primarily from its Human Resources division. The move was part of a strategic shift to automate routine administrative functions through an AI system known as AskHR.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 2023년 약 8,000명 감원(HR 부문 중심), AskHR 자동화 연계 (HR Asia 2차 보도)

> Chief Executive Officer Arvind Krishna explained the development in an interview with The Wall Street Journal, stating, “Our total employment has actually gone up, because what [AI] does is it gives you more investment to put into other areas.”
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 총 고용 확대·재투자 (Krishna WSJ 인터뷰 재인용)

> These areas include software engineering, marketing, and sales—roles that demand creativity, critical thinking, and direct human engagement.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 재투자 대상 직무

> AskHR, which managed over 11.5 million interactions in 2024, significantly improved internal service satisfaction. The platform’s net promoter score (NPS) climbed from -35 to +74 over several years—a testament to its growing acceptance. Still, approximately 6% of queries require human intervention, reinforcing the continued relevance of human judgement in complex or nuanced cases.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AskHR 2024년 11.5M 상호작용, NPS -35→+74, 질의 약 6% 사람 개입

> The results were striking. AskHR automated around 94% of repetitive HR tasks, contributing to a reported $3.5 billion boost in productivity across more than 70 job roles.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 반복 HR 업무 94% 자동화. 단 "$3.5 billion"은 Forbes 기사에서 타 기업 CTO 인용으로 확인되어 IBM 귀속 여부 상충(use case Contradictions 참조)
