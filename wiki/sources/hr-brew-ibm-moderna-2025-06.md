# Source Summary — HR Brew IBM + Moderna AI HR (2025-06-12)

> ⚠ **직접 fetch 실패**: HR Brew가 403으로 차단. WebSearch summary 기반 작성이므로 quotation 정확도는 2차 전달 수준.

## 요약
2025-06-12 HR Brew이 IBM과 Moderna의 HR AI 사용을 나란히 비교·분석. **IBM은 이미 agent 수준**으로 배포, **Moderna는 GPT 기반 interface 수준**이지만 agentic 전환을 "vision for near future"로 선언. 양사 패턴 대조가 이 소스의 핵심 가치.

## IBM HR AI 주장 (Tier 2 전달)

### IBM AskHR 에이전트
- **270,000명 IBM 직원**이 사용
- 정책·보상 등 HR 토픽 전반 답변
- Compensation guidance agent: 매니저에게 "salary budget을 여러 명에게 어떻게 배분할지" 제안
- Expense report troubleshooting 자동화

### IBM watsonx Recognition
- 직원 recognition 메시지를 watsonx가 "meaningful recognition message"로 생성
- 직원이 받은 recognition에 points 부여, rewards marketplace에서 goods 교환 가능

### IBM CEO Arvind Krishna 발언 (WSJ 전달, HR Brew 인용)
- "couple hundred HR workers의 work가 AI agent로 대체됐고, 그 결과 **programmer·salesperson 채용 증가**"

### IBM 내부 직무 전환 예시
- 필리핀 기반 직원: 이전에 전화로 HR 질문 처리 → 현재 **"conversational AI specialist"** 로 전환, AskHR에 prompt engineering 수행
- HR 역할이 "elimin"이 아니라 "elevat" 됐다는 IBM의 프레이밍

## Moderna HR AI 상태 (Tier 2 전달)

### 현재 Total Rewards 도메인 GPT
- Benefits selection 도움
- Equity compensation 이해 ("fewest number of employees understood" 이유로 출시)
- Job leveling

→ [[hr-brew-moderna-total-rewards-2025-05]] (한 달 전 기사)와 동일한 4종 GPT 재확인

### ★ Moderna Patel의 agentic 전환 aspiration
Moderna HR 팀은 "**agents akin to what IBM described**는 아직 쓰지 않고 있지만" **near-future vision**:

> **Patel**: "일부 우리가 이미 하는 반복 업무, 사람이 필요 없는 것들 — interface로 답변만 하는 것이 아니라 **agents가 실제 work를 수행**하는 것으로 가고 싶다"

## Key Quotes

> The company deploys an AI agent called “AskHR” that fields questions from 270,000 individuals on a daily basis about everything from IBM’s maternity leave policy to employment verification letter requests, Moss said during a May 20 presentation at WorldatWork’s Total Rewards conference in Orlando, Florida. Among the total rewards functions the agent can perform is compensation guidance, as the tool can suggest how managers should spend their salary budgets across a certain number of people, and troubleshooting expense reports.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AskHR 270,000명 일 단위 사용, compensation guidance·expense report 기능 (IBM Steve Moss 발표, 자사 보고)

> When an employee wants to leave positive feedback for a colleague, they can answer a series of questions about what they did, which will then prompt IBM’s watsonx software to generate “a meaningful recognition message,” said Beth Sundberg, VP of technology solutions at BI Worldwide, which partnered with IBM on the tool.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: watsonx 기반 recognition 메시지 생성, 파트너 BI Worldwide

> While Patel’s team isn’t yet using agents akin to what IBM described, he said that’s the vision for the near future. The GPTs have helped Moderna’s HR team cut down on email traffic and service tickets, but what his team would really like to start doing is focusing on, “some of the repetitive things that we already do, that don't need a human….that's what an agent is perfect for,” he said. “That’s kind of the next piece for us, is can we get agents actually doing work as opposed to just answering as an interface?” Patel added.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Moderna는 agent 미사용(interface 수준), 이메일·서비스 티켓 감소(정성), agentic 전환은 near-future vision (Hem Patel, VP HR business partner)

> Meanwhile, IBM CEO Arvind Krishna told the Journal that the work of a couple hundred HR workers had been replaced with AI agents, resulting in increased hiring of programmers and salespeople.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Krishna의 "a couple hundred" HR 직원 업무 대체 발언 (WSJ 재인용)

> Moss also said he’d seen examples of HR roles with IBM being “elevated,” such as a Philippines-based employee who previously fielded HR questions via phone, and now works as a “conversational AI specialist,” doing prompt engineering for AskHR.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 필리핀 기반 HR 직원의 conversational AI specialist 전환 사례
