## Summary

Fortune 2024-06-12 (Jason Del Rey). Amazon의 150만 직원 대상 일일 서베이 도구 Connections에 대한 비판적 보도. Amazon은 Connections 데이터를 직원 불만 보도 반박·노조 대응(Bezos 주주서한)에 활용해 왔으나, 다수 직원은 익명성 불신(소규모 팀에서 매니저가 응답자 추정 가능), 매니저의 응답 코칭·압박 등으로 솔직히 답하지 않는다고 증언. Amazon 대변인: 응답은 confidential, 4명 이상 팀에서만 집계 결과 노출, 매니저 응답 영향 시도는 정책 위반. 일일 빈도로 기간별 추세 필터링 가능.

## Key Quotes

> Along the way, the company has invested in programs for its massive, 1.5-million-employee workforce—spread across warehouses and offices around the world—to provide regular feedback on their work experience, including a daily survey tool known as Connections.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 1.5M 직원 대상 daily pulse

> Still, many Amazon employees question the accuracy of the feedback—and thus the data Amazon cites—because they say many workers don’t answer Connections questions honestly.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: critical coverage — 응답 정직성 문제

> Chief among employee concerns is that the anonymity of their responses is not as bulletproof as the company would like to suggest. On small teams for example, employees are concerned that their manager can deduce who responded in what way based on the overall manager-employee relationship. Amazon spokesperson Margaret Callahan said employee responses are confidential and that managers see aggregated results only when a team has at least four employees.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 익명성 skepticism vs Amazon 반박 (4명 이상 팀 집계)

> Callahan added that the daily frequency of the surveying allows managers to filter results for various time frames and view trends for several questions that the tool asks regularly over a period of time.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 일일 빈도의 분석 이점 (Amazon 주장)
