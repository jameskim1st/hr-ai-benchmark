# Lloyds Banking Group wants to train every employee in AI by the end of this year – here's how it plans to do it

## Summary
ITPro(2026-01-23)가 Lloyds Banking Group의 AI Academy 출범을 보도. 67,000명 전 직원에게 역할·직급과 무관하게 '실용적 AI 스킬'을 제공하며 AI Users·Leaders·Builders·Enablers로 학습 경로를 구분, 전원이 'Working with AI Responsibly' 모듈을 먼저 이수. 2026년 말까지 100% 업스킬 목표. 지난해 50개 생성형 AI use case를 프로덕션에 투입하고 2,000만 고객 대상 에이전틱 금융 어시스턴트를 계획 중이라는 맥락 포함.

## Key Quotes
> The bank’s new AI Academy, launched this week, aims to provide “practical AI skills” for 67,000 staff “no matter their role or level”.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 67,000명 AI Academy

> “Learning is tailored to the different ways colleagues use AI from AI Users to AI Leaders, Builders and Enablers,” Lloyds said in a statement. “To begin, all 67,000 colleagues will complete a short module on Working with AI Responsibly, ensuring safe and ethical use aligned with Group standards.”
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 4개 학습 경로·책임 있는 AI 모듈

> Lloyds said it aims to upskill 100% of colleagues by the end of 2026 alone
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 2026 말 100% AI 리터러시 목표

> Last year, the company put 50 generative AI use-cases into full production, ramping up adoption of the technology across core operations, while unveiling plans to deploy an agentic AI-based financial assistant for over 20 million customers.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 2025 GenAI 50개 use case 프로덕션
