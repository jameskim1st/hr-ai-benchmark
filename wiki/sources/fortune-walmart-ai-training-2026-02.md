# Walmart exec says it’s ‘unfortunate’ that other companies are slashing workforces in the name of AI—it’s offering training to 1.6 million workers instead

## Summary

Fortune 기사(2026-02-19). Walmart가 미국·캐나다 프론트라인·사무직 160만 명 전원에게 Google AI Professional Certification 파트너십 기반 8시간 AI 기초 과정을 무료 제공한다고 발표. CPO Donna Morris는 AI로 인력을 대체하는 기업들을 '유감(unfortunate)'이라 표현하며 'people-led, tech-powered' 재편 속 직원 역량 강화를 강조. Google·Ipsos 조사(미국 근로자 40%만 업무 AI 사용, 5% 'AI fluent') 인용.

## Key Quotes

> The retail giant has just announced that its 1.6 million workforce will be provided free AI training.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 160만 직원 무료 AI 교육

> Both frontline and corporate staff in the U.S. and Canada will have access to an eight-hour course on the fundamentals of AI, as part of its partnership with Google’s new AI Professional Certification.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Google AI Professional Certification 8시간 과정

> “We want to make sure that we equip all of our associates with the best tools to allow them to be successful as Walmart continues to reshape as a people-led, tech-powered company,” Morris added.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: People-led, tech-powered 원칙 (CPO 인용)

## Limitations

- 이 기사는 2026-02 Google 인증 파트너십을 다루며, 2025-09 OpenAI 인증 파트너십과는 별개 프로그램 — 사용 사례에서 혼동하지 않도록 주의.
- Walmart 발표·인터뷰 기반.
