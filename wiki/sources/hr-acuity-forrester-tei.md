# HR Acuity Can Deliver ROI of 520 Percent According to Total Economic Impact Study from Independent Research Firm

## Summary
HR Acuity가 의뢰한 Forrester Consulting Total Economic Impact 연구(2019-08) 발표 보도자료. 고객 5개사 인터뷰 기반 composite 조직에서 3년 ROI 520%, 법적 리스크·비용 회피 $2.4M, 전략적 인사이트 효과 $1.1M, ER팀 효율 20% 향상 등을 제시. AI 기능(olivER) 이전 시점의 플랫폼 효과 연구다.

## Key Quotes
> The study found that organizations that deploy HR Acuity can achieve an overall ROI of 520 percent within three years.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Forrester TEI 520% ROI

> To measure the impact of the HR Acuity solution, Forrester created a composite organization based on interviews with five HR Acuity clients.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 방법론: 고객 5개사 composite

> $2.4M of avoided and mitigated legal risk and costs, by helping employee relations teams identify problem areas early and take proactive measures to correct the underlying causes
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 법적 리스크 회피 $2.4M

> 20% improved employee relations team efficacy. The employee relations team spends less time entering data and creating reports, and more time understanding the data. HR and business partner efficacy was also improved by $345K.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: ER팀 효율 20%


## Limitations
벤더 커미션 TEI(2019)로 이후 AI 기능(olivER, 2024~)의 효과를 반영하지 않음. 7년 경과로 stale. composite 조직 기반 추정치이며 실제 고객별 ROI 아님.
