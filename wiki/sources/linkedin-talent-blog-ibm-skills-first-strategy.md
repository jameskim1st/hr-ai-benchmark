## Summary

LinkedIn Talent Blog. IBM VP of IBMer growth and development Kelli Jordan 인터뷰. 2017-10 7명으로 시작한 apprenticeship이 5년간 약 1,000명 채용, $250M 투자 공약(2025년까지). 연평균 학습 86시간, 'super learner'는 신규 직무 이동 가능성 20%↑·승진 40%↑. 직원이 보유 스킬을 파악하고 학습 플랫폼에서 역할별 필요 스킬을 쌓는 도구 구축, internal mobility가 문화의 일부. 스킬 우선 채용의 다양성 효과.

## Key Quotes

> “When we look at the average number of learning hours per year for IBMers, it’s 86. And we know that the people we consider to be super learners are actually 20% more likely to move to a new role and 40% more likely to get a promotion.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 학습 ↔ 내부 이동/승진 상관 (IBM 자사 데이터)

> “We’ve built tools that help IBMers understand the skills they have,” Kelli notes, “and how those skills can be built through our learning platforms so that you can see the skills you need for a role, and you can click into our platform to build a particular skill.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 스킬 프로파일 + 역할별 필요 스킬 매칭 도구

> “Internal mobility and growth,” she says, “is part of our culture. We want IBMers to be in control of their careers, and we want them to be continually finding new opportunities and progressing in their journey.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: internal mobility 문화

> We’ve now hired almost a thousand apprentices in five years.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: apprenticeship 규모
