# IBM AskHR — IBM HR enhances employee experience with IBM watsonx Orchestrate

## Summary
IBM 공식 사례연구. 6년간 고도화된 사내 가상 에이전트 AskHR이 80개 이상 HR 태스크를 자동화하고 연 210만 건 이상 직원 대화를 처리하며, 2025년 watsonx Orchestrate를 통합해 생성형·에이전틱 자동화로 진화. AI가 일상 문의를, 인간 어드바이저가 복잡 건을 맡는 2계층 모델이며 Workday·SAP·Concur와 통합. 4년간 HR 운영비 40% 절감, 94% containment, 2016년 대비 지원 티켓 75% 감소, 2024년 1,150만 건 상호작용을 보고.

## Key Quotes
> Over the past six years, IBM has continuously refined their internal virtual agent, AskHR, to automate more than 80 HR tasks and handle over 2.1 million employee conversations annually. Most recently, in 2025, the team integrated IBM® watsonx Orchestrate® to enhance AskHR’s gen AI and agentic automation capabilities.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 80+ 태스크·연 2.1M 대화·2025 watsonx Orchestrate 통합

> AskHR currently operates on a two-tier support model: AI handles routine inquiries while human advisors manage more complex needs, driving both efficiency and personalized service. Behind the scenes, complex HR processes are streamlined through deep integration with enterprise systems such as Workday, SAP and Concur.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 2계층 지원 모델·시스템 통합

> The AI agent helped contribute to a 40% reduction in the HR team’s operational costs over the past four years. AskHR also achieved a 94% containment rate of common questions, has led to a 75% reduction in support tickets raised since 2016, and created more than 11.5 million employee interactions in 2024 alone.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 운영비 -40%·94% containment·티켓 -75%


## Limitations
