# Deloitte brings Claude to 470,000 people

## Summary
Anthropic 공식 발표(2025-10-06). Deloitte 글로벌 네트워크 470,000명에게 Claude 제공, Claude Center of Excellence 설립, 15,000명 대상 Claude 인증 프로그램 공동 개발. 규제 산업용 컴플라이언스 솔루션을 Deloitte Trustworthy AI 프레임워크와 결합. Anthropic CCO Paul Smith·Deloitte Ranjit Bawa 인용.

## Key Quotes
> Deloitte will make Claude available to 470,000 people across its global network

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 470,000명

> Deloitte and Anthropic are co-creating a formal certification program to train and certify 15,000 of its professionals on Claude.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 15,000명 인증

> These solutions will combine Claude's safety-first design with Deloitte's Trustworthy AI™ framework, so companies can drive increased transparency into model decision making.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Trustworthy AI 프레임워크 결합

> “Deloitte chose Claude because they need trusted AI that can help their employees and clients across industries and on a global scale—from coding and software development to customer engagement and industry-specific advisory,” said Paul Smith, Chief Commercial Officer of Anthropic.
