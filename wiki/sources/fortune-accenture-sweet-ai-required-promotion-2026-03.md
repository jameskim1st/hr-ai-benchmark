## Summary

Fortune 2026-03-13. 'Rapid Response' 팟캐스트에서 Accenture CEO Julie Sweet가 AI 도구 사용을 승진 요건으로 명시('If you want to get promoted, you've got to do the things that we do'). 2025-09 발표한 $865M+ 6개월 optimization 프로그램(수천 명 재스킬링 + 적응 거부자 exit) 재확인. 3년 $3B AI 투자(2023 발표), AI 인력 80,000명 목표, 직원 770,000명+. 'zero to you won't get promoted'가 아니라 3년에 걸친 점진적 전환이라고 설명. Gallup·NBER 데이터로 타 기업 AI 도입 현황 대비.

## Key Quotes

> “If you want to get promoted, you’ve got to do the things that we do in order to operate Accenture,” Sweet said.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Sweet 2026-03: AI 미사용 시 승진 불가

> The company announced in September it has invested more than $865 million in a “six-month business optimization program,” including reskilling thousands of employees—and showing the door to those who refused to adapt to using evolving workplace technology.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: $865M 프로그램 + non-adaptable exit

> “We didn’t go from zero to ‘you won’t get promoted’ in a month. It’s over a three-year period of getting used to the technology, making sure it’s user-friendly, making sure we have the right workbench for people to use, and then saying, ‘Hey, this is Accenture and how we operate.’”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 3년에 걸친 점진적 전환

> The mass reskilling effort is part of Accenture’s three-year, $3 billion push to integrate AI first announced in 2023. One goal of the effort was to double the company’s AI talent to 80,000 professionals through hiring, acquisitions, and training. Accenture has more than 770,000 employees.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: $3B AI 투자, AI 인력 80,000 목표
