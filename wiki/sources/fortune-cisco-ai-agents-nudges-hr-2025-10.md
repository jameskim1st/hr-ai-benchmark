## Summary

Fortune 2025-10-09 (Great Place To Work 기고, Ted Kitterman). Cisco CPO Kelly Jones 인터뷰: HR 팀이 회사 정보·직원 데이터로 HR 질문에 직접 답하는 AI 에이전트를 구축 — 잔여 PTO 조회를 HR case 없이 즉답하고, 휴가 요청을 기록한 뒤 리더에게 보낼 통지 letter 작성까지 제안. 옵트인·데이터 이동 투명성 등 신뢰 확보 강조. 향후 EU 근태 추적, engagement 기반 nudge, 연 10,000 채용 포지션에 대한 개인화 추천, 온보딩·복리후생 개인화, 보상 조정 nudge 계획. 86,000명+ 기업의 5% 시간 회수 논리.

## Key Quotes

> For the HR team at Cisco, one of the basic ways to save employees’ time was to create an AI agent that can directly answer their HR questions using company information and employee data. For example, a question about how much remaining personal time off an employee has gets a quick response without having to open a case with HR.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: HR case 없이 PTO 잔여 즉답 — AI Assistant for HR

> When an employee tells the tool they want to take two weeks off in September, the agent not only logs the request but helps take the next step: “It will say, sure, do you want me to write a letter to your leader letting them know you’re going?” Jones says.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Q&A를 넘어 time-off notification 메시지 작성까지 (agentic)

> “If you can give 5% of their time back to a more than 86,000-person enterprise, what they can do with that time to drive better outcomes for our customers is exponential.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 86,000명+ 규모 시간 회수 논리

> “We fill 10,000 roles a year,” she explains. “Soon AI can go in and give customized nudges to an employee, based on their interest or skills that a role opened up and maybe they should apply.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 향후 internal mobility nudge 계획
