# Amazon plans sweeping HR job cuts as AI investment grows

## Summary
HR Grapevine USA가 Fortune 보도를 인용해 Amazon PXT 조직의 최대 15% 감원 계획을 보도. PXT는 SVP Beth Galetti가 이끌며 10,000명 이상, 채용·HR 운영·기술 기능을 포괄. Amazon은 향후 1년간 AI·클라우드 인프라에 최대 $1,000억 투자 예정이며 Jassy 메모의 "총 corporate workforce 감소" 발언 인용. 동시에 250,000명 계절직 창고 인력 채용 계획 언급.

## Key Quotes
> Amazon is reportedly preparing to trim up to 15% of its human resources (HR) workforce, as accelerated investment in artificial intelligence and data infrastructure sees the e-commerce giant enter a new cost-cutting phase.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: HR 인력 최대 15% 감축

> The PXT unit, led by Senior Vice President Beth Galetti, employs more than 10,000 people globally and encompasses recruiting, HR operations, and technology functions.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: PXT 리더(Beth Galetti)·규모·기능 범위

> “We expect that this will reduce our total corporate workforce as we get efficiency gains from using AI extensively across the company,” Jassy wrote in a company-wide memo.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AI 효율화로 corporate 인력 감소 예고

> Even as corporate teams brace for job losses, Amazon has just announced plans to hire 250,000 seasonal warehouse and logistics workers for the upcoming holiday period.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 250,000명 계절직 채용과의 대비
