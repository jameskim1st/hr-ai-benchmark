# How JPMorgan is Embedding AI into Employee Performance

## Summary
AI Magazine이 FT 보도를 인용해 JPMorgan Chase 직원이 자체 LLM Suite로 연간 성과 리뷰 초안을 작성할 수 있게 됐다고 보도. LLM Suite는 8개월 만에 200,000명 온보딩, 2024-07 임원 메모에서 '리서치 애널리스트'로 비유. 2025년 $18bn 기술 투자 중 AI가 핵심이며 'AI Made Easy' 교육에 수만 명이 참여했다는 Derek Waldron 인터뷰(2024-10-29 McKinsey)를 재인용.

## Key Quotes
> As a result, JPMorgan Chase, led by CEO Jamie Dimon, is advancing its use of AI by offering employees the option to use it for writing annual performance reviews.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 성과 리뷰 초안 작성 기능

> JPMorgan Chase introduced its LLM Suite to employees in early 2024 and reported that it had onboarded 200,000 users within the first eight months.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 8개월 200,000명

> In the memo, JPMorgan Chase said: “Think of LLM Suite as a research analyst that can offer information, solutions and advice on a topic.”
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 내부 메모 포지셔닝

> He also highlights JPMorgan Chase’s ‘AI Made Easy’ training programme, which has already seen involvement from tens of thousands of employees.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AI Made Easy 참여 규모
