## ADP Assist — Key AI Features Announced

### 1. Payroll Anomaly Detection & Resolution
- Automatically identifies inconsistencies/deviations in payroll data
- Suggests corrections for HR practitioners to review and approve (human-in-the-loop)
- **Early adopter metric**: ⚠️ 자사 보고: "Up to 30 minutes saved per payroll cycle" via proactive error prevention
- Learning system: continuously adapts to seasonal variations, org changes, evolving requirements

### 2. Conversational Analytics
- Natural language queries → immediate answers, charts, insights, presentation-ready reports
- Example: "How are our overtime costs trending?" → actionable visualization in seconds

### 3. Compliance Automation
- Real-time regulatory monitoring, automated compliance task support
- Multi-product: Workforce Now® / ADP Global Payroll® / ADP Lyric HCM®
