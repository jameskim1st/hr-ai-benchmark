## Summary

IBM 공식 case study(2023-12 제작). IBM HR ELOA(Enterprise Learning Operations and Administration) Event Management 팀이 2023년 상반기에만 8,000개 학습 이벤트를 운영하며 홍보·등록·출석 추적이 수작업·오류 다발 → watsonx Orchestrate로 AI 어시스턴트 cHaRlie(Cognitive Human Resource Learning EM Assistant) 구축. 등록 모니터링, 저조 등록 alert, 맞춤 홍보, 사전 커뮤니케이션, 가상 클래스 출석 로깅 자동화. 2023 초 출시 후 6개월: 이벤트 후 NPS 기준 만족도 15% 상승, 출석 캡처 100% 정확도, 출석부 업데이트 turnaround 91%+ 개선.

## Key Quotes

> Using watsonx Orchestrate, Hema and her team built an AI assistant for event managers and they called it cHaRlie: Cognitive Human Resource Learning EM Assistant. CHaRlie assists event managers with a range of critical but repetitive, error-prone tasks such as monitoring class enrollment, alerting managers to events that require follow up due to low enrollment, managing customized event promotion, deploying pre-event communications and logging attendance of virtual classes.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: cHaRlie 기능 범위 (watsonx Orchestrate 기반)

> In the first six months after it was launched in early 2023, cHaRlie had a meaningful impact on the event management team and its clients, IBM employees. The entire ELOA team has observed a 15% increase in employee satisfaction (as measured through net promoter score surveys post-event) after cHaRlie was implemented.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: NPS 15% 상승 (자사 보고)

> Additionally, the team reached 100% accuracy in learner attendance capture and achieved at least a 91% improvement in the turnaround time on updating attendance rosters for completed virtual events.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 출석 캡처 100% 정확도, 출석부 turnaround 91% 개선

> In the first half of 2023 alone, the team delivered 8,000 events.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 운영 규모

## Limitations

- IBM이 자사 HR 사례를 자사 제품(watsonx Orchestrate) 홍보용으로 작성 — 벤더 self-case
- 'onboarding time 25% 감소' 수치는 본 case study에 없음 — 출처 불명, 사용 주의
- baseline 수치 미공개, 6개월 초기 효과
