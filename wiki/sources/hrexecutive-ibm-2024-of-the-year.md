# Innovation at IBM: Announcing the 2024 HR Executive of the Year

## Summary
HR Executive가 IBM CHRO Nickle LaMoreaux를 2024 HR Executive of the Year로 선정하며 IBM HR의 AI 전환을 상세 소개. 2017년 AskHR 출시 후 Client Zero로서 80개 자동화 통합, 2023년 AskHR 1,000만 회 이상 상호작용·765,000건 트랜잭션·94% 자체 처리. 승진 프로세스를 간소화하는 AI 디지털 어시스턴트 HiRo가 컨설팅 관리자에게 연 50,000시간을 절감했다고 보도.

## Key Quotes
> The numbers tell the story: In 2023, employees interacted with AskHR more than 10 million times, and the assistant handled 765,000 transactions, successfully fielding 94% of the questions posed to it and transferring the more complex issues to human HR professionals.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AskHR 2023 규모

> Meanwhile, IBM's AI digital assistant HiRo, which is streamlining promotion processes, saved the organization's consulting managers 50,000 hours just last year.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: HiRo 승진 프로세스·50,000시간 절감

> Since LaMoreaux took the reins of HR, she has catapulted IBM's reliance on AskHR while also leading the integration of 80 automations into the HR function's work—generating significant operational savings while boosting productivity, efficiency and engagement.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 80개 자동화

> The year before AskHR was rolled out, those HR professionals were tasked with handling more than 1.5 million employee questions.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Before 기준선


## Limitations
수상 기사 특성상 IBM 측 진술 위주. HiRo '10주 → 단축', 'HRBP 시간 85% 절감', '매니저 10,000명 배포' 수치는 본 기사에 없음(별도 출처 필요). 50,000시간은 컨설팅 관리자 기준.
