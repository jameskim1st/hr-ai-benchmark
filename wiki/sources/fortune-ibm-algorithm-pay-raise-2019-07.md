## Summary

Fortune 2019-07-14 (Anne Fisher). IBM이 2015년부터 구축한 AI 성과관리 시스템: Watson 알고리즘으로 퇴직 가능성이 높은 직원을 예측하고 교육·승진 등 행동을 추천(특허). CHRO Diane Gherson: 특정 그룹에 10% 인상 시 flight risk 90% 감소라는 데이터가 있었고, 이를 따르지 않은 매니저의 팀 attrition은 따른 팀의 2배. 시스템은 추천 사유를 설명해 매니저를 설득('open up the black box'). 매니저가 최종 결정권 유지. McKinsey Bryan Hancock의 성과관리 변화 코멘트.

## Key Quotes

> Using Watson algorithms, the HR team developed and patented a program that looks at patterns in data from all over IBM and predicts which employees are most likely to quit in the near future. The algorithms then recommend actions—like more training or awarding an overdue promotion—to keep them from leaving.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Predictive attrition + 행동 추천

> “At one point, all the data showed that giving a certain group of employees a 10% raise would reduce their ‘flight risk’ by 90%,” says Gherson. “Managers who didn’t take that advice had attrition rates on their teams that were twice as high as for those who did.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AI 보상 추천 무시 시 팀 attrition 2배

> Another way IBM won over skeptical managers, she adds, was by “explaining why the system recommends a certain action. You have to open up the black box a bit and show people the data.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: explainable 추천

> Even so, Gherson maintains that performance management is still “mostly human. Managers know their direct reports better than the algorithms do. They still have the last word.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 매니저 최종 결정권 (human-in-the-loop)

## Limitations

- IBM CHRO 발언 기반 — '10% 인상 → flight risk 90% 감소'는 특정 그룹 사례이며 일반화 불가
- 2019 기사 — 24개월 이상 경과(stale)
- per-employee salary 추천 제품화·2024-26 운영 여부는 본 기사에 없음
