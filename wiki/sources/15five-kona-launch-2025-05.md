## Summary

15Five가 2025년 5월 15Five Next 행사에서 AI 기반 매니저 효과성 코치 'Kona'를 출시. Kona는 1:1 미팅에 자동 참여해 대화를 전사(transcribe)하고, 성과 리뷰·engagement 설문·이전 미팅 맥락을 기반으로 실시간 코칭 팁을 제공. ReUp Education이 초기 도입 고객으로 소개됨.

## Key Quotes

> Kona provides tailored coaching and enablement to managers in native workflows like Slack, Zoom, and Google Meet helping improve critical manager effectiveness skills that increase performance, engagement, and retention
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 사용자 접점·연동 채널이 Slack, Zoom, Google Meet (벤더 주장)

> An AI-powered coach for managers, Kona joins virtual meetings and provides personalized, privacy-conscious coaching and enablement in the flow of work. Kona uses data from 15Five such as performance reviews and engagement surveys alongside the context it builds over time to help people managers continuously level up across a wide range of critical skillsets – from leading more effective meetings to navigating difficult performance conversations.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 화상 미팅 자동 참여, 성과 리뷰·engagement 설문·누적 맥락을 입력으로 사용 (벤더 주장)

> Kona automatically measures changes in manager behavior so HR leaders can see what’s working and what needs rethought in an ongoing way.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 매니저 행동 변화 자동 측정, HR 리더 모니터링 (벤더 주장)

> Kona provides personalized, actionable notes for managers so they are prepared going into 1-1s. Notes include detailed recommendations for ways to better engage and acknowledge participants building on insights from previous meetings.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 1:1 사전 준비 노트와 이전 미팅 기반 권고 생성 (벤더 주장)

> “As a growing company, we’re promoting rockstar individual contributors into management roles, but we can’t scale traditional coaching fast enough,” said Jenn Sobocinski, Senior Director of People and Culture at ReUp Education, an early adopter of Kona. “Kona is helping us support those new managers in real time, with feedback that helps them adjust and grow immediately. It’s also changed how I run my own meetings. This kind of continuous, in-the-flow coaching is exactly what fast-growing companies need.”
