## Summary

CNBC 2019-04-03, CNBC @Work Talent + HR Summit에서 IBM CEO Ginni Rometty 발언. Watson 기반 특허 'predictive attrition program'이 퇴직 예정 직원을 95% 정확도(IBM 공식: 95% 'range')로 예측하고 매니저 행동을 처방, 약 $300M 리텐션 비용 절감 주장. HR 부서 규모 30% 축소. 매니저 서베이보다 데이터 기반 스킬 추론이 정확. MYCA(My Career Advisor)와 Blue Match(옵트인, AI 추론 스킬 기반 직무 추천) 소개 — 2018년 신규 직무·승진을 받은 IBM 직원 27% 중 일부가 Blue Match 지원. 연간 성과평가 폐지, 분기 체크인.

## Key Quotes

> IBM artificial intelligence technology is now 95 percent accurate in predicting workers who are planning to leave their jobs, said Rometty.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 95% attrition 예측 정확도 (IBM CEO 주장)

> IBM HR has a patent for its "predictive attrition program" which was developed with Watson to predict employee flight risk and prescribe actions for managers to engage employees.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Watson 기반 특허 Predictive Attrition Program

> "It took time to convince company management it was accurate," Rometty said, but the AI has so far saved IBM nearly $300 million in retention costs, she claimed.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: $300M 리텐션 절감 (자사 주장)

> Its companion, Blue Match technology, serves up job openings to employees based on their AI-inferred skills data (employees opt into the service). Rometty said some of the 27 percent of IBM workers who received a new job or promotion in 2018 were assisted by Blue Match.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Blue Match: 옵트인, AI 추론 스킬 기반 직무 추천

> "We found manager surveys were not accurate," Rometty said, referring to formal skills assessments. "Managers are subjective in ratings. We can infer and be more accurate from data."

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 데이터 기반 스킬 추론 근거

## Limitations

- IBM CEO 발언 전달 — 95%·$300M 모두 IBM 자사 주장, 'secret sauce' 비공개로 독립 검증 불가
- 2019 기사 — 24개월 이상 경과, 현재 운영 status 불확실
- 34+ 변수·overtime 변수·6개월 예측 기간 등 세부는 본 기사에 없음
