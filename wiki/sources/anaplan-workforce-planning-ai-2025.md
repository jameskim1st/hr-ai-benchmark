## AI Agents Announced (December 2025)

- **Workforce Analyst agent**: Identifies workforce risks, quantifies headcount decision impact, answers real-time workforce planning questions
- **CoModeler agent**: Natural language → structured model logic (minutes vs. days/weeks)
- **Broader agent suite**: Analyze data, generate narrative reports, recommend and execute approved actions (e.g., resource reallocation)
