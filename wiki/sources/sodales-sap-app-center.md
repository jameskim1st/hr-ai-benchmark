# Sodales announced as the first Premium Certified Endorsed App with SAP GENAI Hub and SAP AI Core

## Summary

Sodales Solutions 보도자료(2024-10-22, 토론토). Sodales가 SAP GenAI Hub·SAP AI Core를 활용하고 Employee Central과 통합되는 '첫 Premium Certified Endorsed App' 지위를 획득했다고 발표. SAP Business AI로 안전·노동 규제 준수와 노사 분쟁 예방을 지원한다는 SAP Darryl Gray(GVP, Software Partner Innovation Success) 인용. 사용 사례가 'SAP App Center' 등재 정보로 인용한 항목이나, SAP App Center 도메인은 폐지되어 이 보도자료로 대체.

## Key Quotes

> Toronto, ON – October 22, 2024 – Sodales is thrilled to announce its official status as the First Premium Certified Endorsed App utilizing the SAP GENAI Hub and SAP AI Core, seamlessly integrating with Employee Central.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: SAP Business AI(GenAI Hub) 활용 첫 Premium Certified Endorsed App

> “By harnessing the power of SAP Business AI for safety and labor regulatory compliance, organizations can proactively mitigate risks and instill a culture of accountability,” said Darryl Gray, Global Vice President – SAP Software Partner Innovation Success.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: SAP 측 확인 인용

> Sodales addresses these challenges by offering an Enterprise Health, Safety and Employee Relations software enabled by SAP Business AI.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 제품 포지셔닝(EHS + ER, SAP Business AI)

## Limitations

- 벤더 자체 보도자료(Tier 3). 원래 참조 대상인 SAP App Center 등재 페이지(https://www.sapappcenter.com/apps/6659/overview)는 도메인 자체가 해석되지 않고(DNS 실패), 후속 SAP Store 페이지(https://store.sap.com/product/display-0000006659_live_v1/...)는 JS 전용으로 본문 확보 불가.
- 'Industry Cloud Solutions Portfolio' 문구는 이 자료에 없음(2022-06 Sodales 보도자료 'Premium Certified as an SAP Endorsed App, available for purchase on the SAP store'는 raw/vendors/sodales-premium-certification-sap-endorsed-app.md에 별도 스냅샷).
