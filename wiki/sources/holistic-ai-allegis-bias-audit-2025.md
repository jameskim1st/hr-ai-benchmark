- **Solution**: Holistic AI centralized risk registry + continuous monitoring across AI lifecycle

## Hired (job marketplace) — NYC Local Law 144 Compliance

- **Deployed**: Holistic AI bias audit (independent third party) ahead of LL144 effective date (2023-07-05)
- **Scope**: Adverse Impact and Disparate Impact testing on Hired's matching/ranking AEDT
- **Outcome**: Audit report + Summary of Results published; ongoing annual audit cadence established
- **Status**: Compliant; under Holistic AI's Ongoing Assurance program (continuous legislative updates + annual re-audits)
- **Hired scale**: 10,000+ partner companies, matching active candidates globally

## NYC LL144 Regulatory Context (2025–2026)
- December 2025 NY State Comptroller audit found DCWP enforcement "ineffective" — at least 17 instances of non-compliance missed out of 32 companies reviewed
- Enforcement intensification expected 2026; penalties $500–$1,500/day per violation
- August 2026: EU AI Act high-risk system compliance deadline (HR AI tools classified as high-risk under Annex III)
