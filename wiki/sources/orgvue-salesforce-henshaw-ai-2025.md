## Summary

Orgvue launched Henshaw AI in December 2025 — a suite of platform capabilities automating workforce data analysis for org design and strategic workforce planning. A Salesforce testimonial is the most specific named customer reference. Orgvue also published research (2025) on AI workforce transformation decisions. Both are Tier 3/4 (vendor PR + self-funded research); no independent Tier 1/2 analysis of Salesforce-specific claims found.

## Henshaw AI Platform Capabilities

- **Henshaw Roles**: Automates role clustering and job architecture — reduces manual effort from weeks/months to minutes
- **CoModeler**: Natural language → structured models and calculations (minutes vs. days/weeks)
- **Workforce Analyst AI agent**: Real-time workforce risk identification, headcount impact communication

## Orgvue 2025 AI Workforce Research

- 42% of organizations "testing or researching" AI for workforce deployment
- **32%** of organizations that made redundancies on "cost-saving promise of AI" had to rehire staff
- **55%** admitted it was the wrong decision to make people redundant following AI implementation
- 23% based layoff decisions on "general assumptions about AI capabilities" rather than role-specific analysis
