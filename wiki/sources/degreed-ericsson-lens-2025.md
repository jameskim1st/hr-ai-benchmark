## Summary

Degreed announced Degreed Maestro and a suite of AI learning tools at LENS 2025 (April 15, 2025), with Ericsson cited as a lead customer reference for skills-first AI strategy. Bayer AG and International Motors also named as early-access customers for Maestro. Coverage via BusinessWire (Tier 4/vendor); HR Executive named Degreed Maestro a 2025 Top HR Product (Tier 2). Ericsson-specific data from Degreed microsite (Tier 3).
