# The ROI of BetterUp: Impactful Change that Scales

## Summary
BetterUp의 ROI 소개 페이지. Twilio(8,000+ 직원 전원 대상)가 2년 후 분석한 결과 코칭 받은 직원은 고성과 평가 가능성 32% 높고 이탈 가능성 5배 낮음. Chipotle는 코칭 직원 승진율 1.2x(본사)·1.8x(현장). 그 외 영업 매니저 팀 quota 달성 1.6x, 4.3x 낮은 attrition으로 $14M 절감 등 고객별 수치 나열.

## Key Quotes
> On a mission to create a business that can take on any challenge that comes its way, Twilio engaged BetterUp to provide its leaders with coaching, ultimately rolling the program out to all 8,000+ employees.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Twilio 8,000+ 전직원 롤아웃

> Two years into its work with BetterUp, Twilio analyzed the impact of coaching on the effectiveness of its employees and found coached employees were 32% more likely to receive high performance ratings and 5x less likely to leave the organization than their non-coached peers.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 고성과 평가 32%↑·이탈 5배↓

> With Chipotle’s BetterUp partnership, manager effectiveness skyrocketed. Employees using BetterUp received higher performance ratings and were promoted at higher rates than those without—corporate employees received 1.2x more promotions while field leaders received 1.8x more promotions.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Chipotle 승진율 1.2x/1.8x


## Limitations
