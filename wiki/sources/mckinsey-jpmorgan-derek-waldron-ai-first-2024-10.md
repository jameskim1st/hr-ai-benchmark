# JPMorgan Chase’s Derek Waldron on AI and banking

## Summary
McKinsey가 JPMorgan Chase Chief Analytics Officer Derek Waldron과 진행한 인터뷰. 전 직원 대상 AI 교육 프로그램 'AI Made Easy'를 브랜드화해 수만 명이 수강했고, 사내 스크린·타운홀 캠페인으로 사용을 유도. 프롬프트 엔지니어→컨텍스트 엔지니어, 지식관리 등 신규 직무군의 출현과 기존 SW 엔지니어·데이터 과학자의 업스킬 필요성, 프롬프트 라이브러리·'prompt of the week' 등 동료 학습을 설명한다.

## Key Quotes
> We branded a training program, AI Made Easy, at scale, and we continue to update that training. It resonated and tens of thousands of employees took it. We launched marketing campaigns through JPMorgan Chase screens, management channels, and town halls to drive people to use these tools.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: AI Made Easy 프로그램·수만 명 수강

> One of the first was the prompt engineer—a whole new category of people who weren’t software engineers or data scientists but who could understand how to convey business logic or objectives in a language that LLMs can understand and execute. The prompt engineer is evolving into what we would call a context engineer
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 신규 직무군 출현

> Our AI training starts simple and grows in sophistication. The first phase: what an LLM can and cannot do. Second: types of instructions or questions. Once there’s familiarity with capabilities, we move into how to construct good prompts, with frameworks and examples and constraints.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 커리큘럼 구조

> At JPMorgan, many teams quickly set up prompt libraries, “prompt of the week” emails, and social channels to share power-user innovations. Word of mouth is one of the best training channels.
→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 동료 학습 메커니즘
