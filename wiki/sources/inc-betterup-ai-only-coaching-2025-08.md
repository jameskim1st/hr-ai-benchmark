# How BetterUp Built an AI-Only Coaching Product 95 Percent of Its Customers Love

## Summary
Inc.의 Annabel Burba가 2025-08-18 게재한 기사. BetterUp(2021년 기업가치 $4.7 billion) CEO Alexi Robichaux가 2021년부터 개발한 AI-only 코칭 제품 'BetterUp Grow'를 2025-01 발표, 2025-04 공개 출시. 첫 버전 "Miranda"(voice-to-text)는 human coach 수준에 못 미쳐 미출시, 2023년 GPT-4 등장 후 standalone 제품 개발 재개. 개발 시간 대부분을 안전 프로토콜·가드레일에 투입. 회사 대변인 기준 초기 테스트 고객 만족 95%, 자신감(confidence) 16% 향상. 매출·수익성은 비공개. 2026-10-19 Aside로 원문 확보

## Key Quotes

> That’s why BetterUp didn’t release its AI coaching platform BetterUp Grow until this April—although the Austin-based company began developing the technology in 2021.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: Grow 개발 시작 2021, 출시 2025-04

> Once GPT-4 came out in 2023, Robichaux recalls thinking the technology was finally sophisticated enough to allow BetterUp to create a standalone AI coaching offering. His team’s attention then turned to safety protocols and guardrails. Robichaux notes that his team spent most of their time on these aspects of the AI-only product because enterprises value knowing these tools are “trustworthy and safe” above all else.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 개발 시간 대부분을 안전 가드레일에 투입 (⚠️ 벤더 주장)

> The company announced Grow, after nearly two more years of development and testing, in January 2025, and then publicly launched it in April. Early testing seems promising; customers report 95 percent satisfaction rates and a 16 percent increase in confidence after using the tool, according to a company spokesperson.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 고객 만족 95%, 자신감 16% 향상 (⚠️ 벤더 주장, 회사 대변인 전달)

> Sales are growing too. Robichaux reports that BetterUp has seen “massive” member growth since debuting this product, as well as “a ton of interest” among existing members who use BetterUp’s other coaching tools. The company declined to share revenue metrics and profitability status.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 성장은 정성 서술만, 매출·수익성 비공개
