# ServiceNow Customer Story — Siemens AG (cs-siemens-ag.pdf)

## Summary

ServiceNow의 Siemens AG 고객 사례 PDF(5쪽, 문서 표기 SN_CaseStudy_Siemens AG_Feb_2023). Siemens GBS(Global Business Services)가 HR·재무 공유서비스를 ServiceNow HR Service Delivery·App Engine·Integration Hub로 통합한 사례. Siemens 직원 311,000명(2022-09-30 기준), 180개국 이상, GBS 11개 주요 delivery location, 연간 송장 10 million건·급여명세서 six million건·주문 700,000건 처리. 이메일·챗봇·음성·전화의 디지털 상호작용 계층, "bionic agent" 프로세스 자동화, 가이드형 온보딩 워크플로, Integration Hub 케이스 라우팅, Amazon·Microsoft Teams 전화 연동. GBS CIO Matthias Egelhaaf 인용: 사용자 만족도 8.8/10, 직원 87% 업무 만족, 자동화 누적 1M 시간. 표지 타일 "1M · 87% · 15K"(자동화 시간·만족도·월 자동 해결 티켓). 2026-10-19 Jina로 원문 확보

## Key Quotes

> The long-term strategy of GBS at Siemens AG has been committed to moving from a workforce-led organization to one that is technology-led. This has required leveraging AI and automation to deliver more efficient services, greater value to the business, and an enhanced experience at a global level.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: GBS의 technology-led 전환 전략 (⚠️ 벤더 사례)

> “I saw that we could collect all our backbone systems, incident and change management, all the typical service management processes of a shared service center, and move them into ServiceNow,” Matthias adds. “We could also create a really modern digital interaction layer incorporating email, chatbot, voice, and telephony.”

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: ServiceNow 이전 범위와 사용자 접점(이메일·챗봇·음성·전화)

> Process automation through a ‘bionic agent’ is also enhancing the employee experience, accelerating response times, reducing errors, and ensuring that the GBS and Hire-to-Retire experience is available in multiple languages.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: bionic agent 자동화와 다국어 제공 (⚠️ 벤더 사례, 기술 세부 없음)

> “It’s all about impact. ServiceNow is more than just providing a robot, it’s solving a real business challenge, and we are now really measuring the results,” says Matthias, adding that Siemens GBS has already achieved one million automated hours, contributing to business productivity and competitiveness based on multiple technologies.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 자동화 누적 1M 시간 (⚠️ 자사 보고, "multiple technologies" 기준이라 ServiceNow 단독 효과 아님)

> “We’re seeing continuous improvements in both employee and user satisfaction,” Matthias notes. “User satisfaction is 8.8 out of 10, and 87% of employees are feeding back that they are enjoying their work. That’s a really good signal.

→ 뒷받침하는 주장: 사용자 만족도 8.8/10, 직원 87% 업무 만족 (⚠️ 자사 보고)
